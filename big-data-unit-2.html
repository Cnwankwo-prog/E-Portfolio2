Unit 2: Reflection on Data Types and Formats

Reflection

Completing the required and recommended reading for Unit 2 strengthened my understanding of the importance of selecting appropriate data types and formats for different analytical tasks. Sardar and Pandey (2024) highlighted that the way data is represented affects how efficiently it can be stored, managed and processed within big data environments. This helped me appreciate that decisions about data format are not simply technical choices, as they can influence system performance, scalability and the accessibility of information.

The reading also developed my understanding of the differences between structured, semi-structured, quasi-structured and unstructured data. I recognised that organisations work with many forms of information, including databases, documents, JSON and XML files, images and web content. Each format has different characteristics and may require different storage, processing and analytical methods.

McKinney (2022) helped connect these concepts to the practical use of Python. Chapters 3 and 6 introduced Python data structures and methods of reading and writing information in different formats. This reinforced that effective analysis begins with understanding how data is organised and selecting suitable tools for importing, manipulating and preparing it.

Overall, the reading increased my confidence in recognising different data formats and considering which format would be most appropriate for a particular task. It also reinforced the importance of interoperability, data quality and effective data preparation. I will apply this learning in future activities by considering the structure and format of data before selecting the methods and tools used to analyse it.

Reading

Sardar, T.H. and Pandey, B.K. (2024) Big Data Computing. Boca Raton, FL: CRC Press, Chapter 8, sections 8.29 and 8.35.

McKinney, W. (2022) Python for Data Analysis: Data Wrangling with Pandas, NumPy, and Jupyter. 3rd edn. Sebastopol, CA: O'Reilly Media, Chapters 3 and 6.
