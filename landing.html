Numerical Analysis

This section presents my learning activities, practical work and reflections from the Numerical Analysis module. It demonstrates my developing understanding of numerical and statistical methods and their application to data analysis and decision-making.

Module Units

Unit 1: Types and Sources of Data and an Introduction to R

This unit introduced the key concepts, methods and tools used to explore datasets in R. I developed experience examining dataset structure, identifying variable types and interpreting basic data characteristics.

  • View Unit 1

Unit 2: Frequency Analysis Using R

This unit developed my understanding of frequency analysis and the use of R to create variables, calculate frequencies, proportions and percentages, and interpret patterns within data.

  • View Unit 2

Unit 3: Data Management in R

This unit focused on managing and transforming data in R, including examining datasets and variables, converting variable types and creating categorical data.

For the practical activity, I used the COVID-19 India dataset to create a binary variable for death reporting, classify confirmed cases and identify reporting patterns across States and Union Territories.

  • View Unit 3

Unit 4: Probabilities, Probability Distributions and Sampling

This unit explored probability and its role in statistical analysis and decision-making. I examined the fundamental rules of probability, discrete and continuous probability distributions and different approaches to sampling.

The unit also developed my understanding of the binomial, Poisson and hypergeometric distributions, as well as the normal, uniform and exponential distributions. I also explored quantiles, the Central Limit Theorem and Type I and Type II errors.

The interactive activities helped me understand how changing distribution parameters affects probability and reinforced the importance of considering data type, sampling method, sample size and statistical error when interpreting numerical evidence.

  • View Unit 4
