Unit 7: Health Data Analysis Using R

Summary

In this activity, I used R to explore a health dataset using descriptive statistics, data visualisation and hypothesis testing. I calculated the mean, median and mode of systolic blood pressure (SBP), diastolic blood pressure (DBP) and income, and used a five-number summary and boxplot to examine the distribution of income.

I also performed a Welch two-sample t-test to investigate whether mean SBP differed according to peptic ulcer status. Although mean SBP was higher among participants with peptic ulcer (131.32 mmHg) than those without (126.86 mmHg), the difference was not statistically significant (p = 0.2296).

This activity strengthened my understanding of how descriptive and inferential statistical techniques can be applied and interpreted in R.
