About Me

Professional Background

I am a pharmacist and healthcare leader with experience across community, mental health and specialist hospital pharmacy. I currently work as a Deputy Chief Pharmacist within the NHS, where my role combines clinical practice, strategic leadership, medicines optimisation, digital transformation and service improvement.

A significant area of my career has been digital healthcare and medicines optimisation. I have experience leading and supporting the development and implementation of Electronic Prescribing and Medicines Administration (EPMA) and Electronic Patient Record (EPR) systems.

This experience has developed my interest in how technology and data can be used to improve patient safety, clinical decision-making, operational efficiency and the overall quality of healthcare services.

Qualifications & Professional Development

My academic and professional development spans clinical practice, leadership, management and digital healthcare. My qualifications include:

  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (BPharm)
  • Master of Science (MSc)
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA)
  • Postgraduate Certificate (PGCert)
  • Independent Prescribing qualification
  • Chartered Manager and Fellow of the Chartered Management Institute (CMgr FCMI)

My Data Science Journey

I am developing my interest in digital healthcare further through postgraduate study in Data Science. This is enabling me to build knowledge and practical skills in statistical analysis, R, Python, data management, big data technologies and data visualisation.

I am particularly interested in moving beyond simply collecting healthcare data to understanding how it can be analysed and translated into meaningful information that supports decision-making and service improvement.

About This E-Portfolio

This e-portfolio documents my learning and development throughout my Data Science studies. It brings together practical activities, academic discussions, projects and reflective learning.

The portfolio demonstrates both the technical skills I am developing and my ability to consider how data science methods can be applied to real-world problems.

Professional Goals

My aim is to combine my experience in pharmacy, clinical practice, digital healthcare and senior leadership with advanced data and analytical skills. I hope to use this combination of expertise to contribute to evidence-based decision-making, digital innovation and data-driven improvement within healthcare.
