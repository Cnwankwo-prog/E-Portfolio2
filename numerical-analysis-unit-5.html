Unit 5: Producing Plots and Introducing Calculus

Summary and Reflection

Using the COVID-19 India dataset, I created four visualisations in R to explore different aspects of the data. The bar chart showed that Kerala had the highest frequency of reports, while the pie chart showed that most observations contained between 1–5 confirmed cases. The histogram demonstrated that recovery numbers were concentrated at the lower end, and the line chart showed that confirmed cases remained relatively low during February before increasing rapidly throughout March.

This activity improved my understanding of how different visualisations can be selected depending on the type of data being analysed. It also developed my confidence in using R functions such as barplot(), pie(), hist() and plot() to identify and communicate patterns within a dataset.
