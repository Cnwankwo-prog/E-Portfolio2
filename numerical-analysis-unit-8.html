Unit 8: Nonparametric Tests

Summary and Reflection

In this activity, I used R to analyse a health dataset using descriptive statistics and nonparametric tests. I applied the Wilcoxon rank-sum test to compare diastolic blood pressure by diabetes status and the Kruskal-Wallis test to compare systolic blood pressure across occupational groups. Neither analysis identified a statistically significant difference. This activity strengthened my understanding of how nonparametric tests can be applied and interpreted in R when comparing groups.
