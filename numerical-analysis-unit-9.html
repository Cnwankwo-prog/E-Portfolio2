Unit 9: Cross-Tabulation Analysis

Data Activity 7

In this activity, I used R to create categorical variables from the COVID-19 India dataset and applied cross-tabulation to explore relationships between geographical region and case severity, and between case severity and recovery status. This activity strengthened my understanding of how cross-tabulation can be used to identify and interpret patterns between categorical variables.

Collaborative Discussion – Reflection

This collaborative discussion strengthened my understanding of how p-values and confidence intervals should be interpreted within the wider context of statistical analysis. Engaging with peer contributions helped me recognise the importance of considering the structure of the data, statistical assumptions and possible confounding factors when interpreting results.

The discussion also developed my understanding of how repeated observations and time can influence apparent relationships within datasets. It reinforced the importance of considering effect sizes, uncertainty and practical relevance rather than relying on statistical significance alone.

Overall, the activity encouraged me to take a more critical approach to statistical outputs and to consider the underlying data and analytical methods before drawing conclusions.
