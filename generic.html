Deciphering Big Data

This e-portfolio presents evidence of my learning, practical activities, collaborative work and professional development throughout the Deciphering Big Data module.

Module Learning Outcomes

  1. LO1: Identify and manage challenges, security issues and risks, limitations and opportunities in data wrangling.
  2. LO2: Critically analyse data-wrangling problems and determine appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques for preparing, cleaning, exploring, creating, optimising and evaluating big data.
  3. LO3: Design, develop and evaluate solutions for processing datasets and solving complex problems using relevant programming paradigms.
  4. LO4: Develop the skills required to work effectively as a member of a development team in a virtual professional environment.
