Unit 4: Data Cleaning and Transformation

Summary

Unit 4 focused on data cleaning and transformation using Python and Pandas. During the practical activities, I worked with survey datasets to examine their structure, interpret metadata, improve variable headings, investigate missing and non-applicable values and examine different data types.

The activities strengthened my understanding of the importance of preparing and validating data before analysis. In particular, I learned that missing values and other apparent data-quality issues should be investigated within the context of the dataset rather than being automatically removed. The activities also demonstrated the value of metadata in making complex datasets easier to understand and analyse.
