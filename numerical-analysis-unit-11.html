Unit 11: Regression Analysis

Data Activity 9

In this activity, I used simple linear regression in R to investigate whether systolic blood pressure (SBP) could be used to predict diastolic blood pressure (DBP). I interpreted the regression equation, coefficient, statistical significance and model fit, and produced a scatterplot with a fitted regression line.

The model identified a statistically significant positive relationship between systolic and diastolic blood pressure and explained approximately 71.7% of the variation in DBP. This activity developed my understanding of how regression can be used to model relationships between variables and how regression outputs should be interpreted.
