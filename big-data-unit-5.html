Unit 5: Case Study on Data Investigations

This unit explored real-world data investigations and the processes involved in identifying and working with appropriate datasets. It considered the importance of data quality, availability, reliability and provenance when preparing data for analysis.

Reflection

This unit helped me understand that working with real-world datasets involves more than simply analysing the available data. The case-study approach highlighted the importance of considering data availability, reliability, provenance and the credibility of the original source before beginning analysis.

The supporting reading also developed my understanding of how Python, NumPy and Jupyter can be used to explore and manipulate data, while data pipelines provide a structured approach for moving and processing data efficiently (McKinney, 2022; Sardar and Pandey, 2024).

Overall, the unit reinforced the importance of assessing data quality and understanding its source before drawing conclusions from an analysis.

References

McKinney, W. (2022) Python for Data Analysis: Data Wrangling with Pandas, NumPy, and Jupyter. 3rd edn. Sebastopol, California: O'Reilly.

Sardar, T.H. and Pandey, B.K. (2024) Big Data Computing. Boca Raton, FL: CRC Press.
