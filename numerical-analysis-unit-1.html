Unit 1: Types and Sources of Data and an Introduction to R

Summary

Unit 1 introduced the use of R to explore and understand datasets. Using a COVID-19 dataset, I examined its structure, identified variable types, converted dates and used basic R functions to answer questions about the data. The activity developed my confidence in preparing and interpreting data before further statistical analysis.
