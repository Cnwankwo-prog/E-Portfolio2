Unit 10: Correlation and Regression

Correlation between Systolic and Diastolic Blood Pressure

In this activity, I used Pearson's correlation and a scatter plot to investigate the relationship between systolic and diastolic blood pressure. The analysis identified a strong positive correlation, indicating that higher systolic blood pressure tended to be associated with higher diastolic blood pressure. This activity strengthened my understanding of how correlation coefficients and visualisation can be used together to examine the strength and direction of relationships between continuous variables.
