Unit 6: Collaborative Discussion – Summary Post

Summary and Reflection

This summary post reflects on how the collaborative discussion developed my interpretation of Brown's data. Peer contributions highlighted the importance of checking underlying counts and denominators, considering how visualisation choices can influence interpretation, and looking beyond presentation to the practical meaning of the findings.

This activity strengthened my understanding that effective data visualisation requires transparency, careful scrutiny of the underlying data and appropriate interpretation. It also demonstrated the value of peer discussion in identifying alternative interpretations and developing a more critical approach to numerical information.
